# Game rules

## Roles and turns

Play with 4–8 people on two teams, with at least two people per team. Each person uses their own iPhone. The host also plays. One random player per team is the receiver (guesser); other players are transmitters (hint givers). Theme names change the role labels, not the rules.

Both teams share one secret answer each round. Only hint givers see it during play. Teams alternate opening rounds. Within a round, hint giving alternates teams; each team's transmitters rotate in roster order.

After each written hint, the opposing receiver guesses or passes first. If that does not finish the round, the owning receiver guesses or passes. The current hint and history remain visible during both decisions.

## Hints and guesses

Hints must be one word, up to 40 characters. Letters, internal hyphens, and apostrophes are allowed. Spaces, digits, empty hints, the answer itself, accepted aliases, and close spellings of the answer are rejected. The game cannot determine semantic cheating: agree as a group not to give spelling codes or parts of a multiword answer.

Guesses must contain at least one letter or number and be at most 80 characters. The player confirms before submitting. Matching ignores capitalization, punctuation, spacing, and a leading “a,” “an,” or “the.” It accepts listed aliases and simple plural variants. For answers/guesses longer than five characters, one insertion, deletion, or substitution is tolerated; transpositions are not a separate special case.

A pass costs nothing. A wrong guess adds one Static. Reaching the configured limit (two or three) immediately gives the round to the other team. A correct guess immediately wins the round. Each round is worth one point. Match settings are first to two, three, or four wins (“best of” three, five, or seven).

## Pacing and interruptions

The host checks verbally that everyone is ready before starting the clue phase and controls when the next round starts. Anyone may leave; the host may return the group to the lobby. A player disconnecting during a match resets scores and returns the remaining group to the lobby. Missing roles are never automatically reassigned mid-round, because that could reveal the answer to a new guesser.

There are no automatic timers, host migration, or saved matches. Restarting the app requires joining/creating a room again.
